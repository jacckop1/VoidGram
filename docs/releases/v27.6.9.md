VoidGram 27.6.9

New
- VoidGram bubbles: everyone with a VoidGram badge now gets the signature grey bubble with the logo. The developer's messages get a gold frame. In light themes the bubble is light, so text stays readable in any theme. Timed badges from the roulette don't give a bubble.
- Classic design: folder tabs as in Telegram before Liquid Glass — text with an underline.
- "Send as a video message" and "Send as a voice message" now work for any video and audio, even if the file isn't downloaded yet: it downloads on its own. The video message is taken from the centre of the video, up to 60 seconds.

Fixed
- Bubbles no longer shift when the long-press menu opens.

Support: @voidgram_sup_bot. Its AI helper answers questions about VoidGram and understands screenshots. You can also send a bug report or a question to the developers there.
